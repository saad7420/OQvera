How each entry is calculated

Paid in USD: amounts are dollars Paid in PKR: amounts are rupees, ÷ rate for $ PKR at today's rate: price in $, client pays $ × today's rate in rupees Revenue = pieces × price + adjustment Worker pay $ = worker pay Rs ÷ entry rate Profit = revenue − fees − worker pay Allah's share = profit × share % (0 if loss) Net profit = profit − Allah's share Salary net = gross − tax − other deductions Transfer = total leaves the account (fee is part of it); the other account receives what arrived
