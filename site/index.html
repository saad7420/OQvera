About me

I'm an AI/ML engineer who builds systems that do real work: agents that answer calls and qualify leads, retrieval pipelines that cite their sources, and computer-vision models that run behind a real API.

Most of my work sits where models meet production — orchestration with LangGraph, retrieval with FAISS and ChromaDB, inference behind FastAPI with queues and caching, and the automations (Make, n8n, Vapi) that connect it all to the tools a business already uses.

I also founded OQVERA, a small studio where I design AI automation and agent systems for businesses.

How I work with clients

I start from the business problem, then build the smallest system that measurably helps — and grow it from there.

  1. Step 1

    Discover

    Map the workflow, the bottleneck and the tools you already use.

  2. Step 2

    Design

    Plan the architecture, what the AI decides and what stays deterministic.

  3. Step 3

    Build

    Develop, integrate and test against real data, then deploy.

  4. Step 4

    Improve

    Monitor how it performs and tighten it with each iteration.
