Smart FAQ Chatbot

Retrieval-first FAQ bot that refuses to invent answers

Searches a knowledge base with embeddings and answers from what it finds — and falls back to a contact message when nothing is relevant enough.

The problem

Support FAQ bots that answer everything end up answering wrong. A wrong answer costs more trust than "I don't know".

How it works

Questions and answers are embedded and indexed in FAISS. A query retrieves the top three matches and keeps only those under the distance threshold; the generator builds context from them, and if none qualify the fallback hands over to a human contact.

What it does

  • Semantic search over a JSON knowledge base
  • Threshold-based relevance filter
  • Graceful hand-off to human support
  • Modular retriever, generator and fallback
